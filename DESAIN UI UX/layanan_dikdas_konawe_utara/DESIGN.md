## Brand & Style
This design system establishes a high-trust, authoritative, yet strikingly modern public sector SaaS experience for primary education governance (SI-DIKDAS Kabupaten Konawe Utara). It translates civic responsibility and national educational pride (*Tut Wuri Handayani*) into a state-of-the-art administrative platform that balances institutional credibility with effortless digital access for educators, school heads, regency officials, and parents.

### Design Movement & Influence
The system bridges **Modern Civic Institutionalism** and **Sleek Glassmorphic SaaS**:
- **Curved Sky-Gradient Canvases:** Directly adopting the expansive, radiant blue-sky ambiance from the reference model, framing complex educational metrics inside friendly, buoyant, forward-moving compositions.
- **Dimensional Floating Glass:** Multi-depth surface panels, subtle translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` / `rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08)`), and frosted backdrops that make administrative data sets feel breathable, organized, and lightweight.
- **Vibrant Civic Accents:** Deep marine navy foundations anchored with official Indonesian educational golden yellow highlights, providing clear visual urgency, verified status signatures, and positive metric indicators.

## Colors
The palette derives from the national educational emblem harmonized with maritime North Konawe tones:

- **Primary Cyan (`#0084C9`):** The primary engine of the interface. Symbolizes forward-looking innovation, skyward potential, and institutional clarity. Applied across primary actions, active navigation states, interactive toggles, and hero highlights.
- **Deep Navy (`#0A2540`):** The governing anchor. Evokes security, ministerial authority, and formal structure. Used for dense data tables, top navigation headers, primary headline text, and solid contrast container elements.
- **Radiant Accent Gold (`#FFC72C`) & Amber Dark (`#D97706`):** The *Tut Wuri Handayani* heritage golden flame. Leveraged strategically for key callouts, high-priority status indicators, merit badges, performance stars, and warning alerts.
- **Crisp Canvas (`#F4F8FC`):** The ambient light canvas tinted with faint sky-blue undertones, avoiding the sterility of flat gray while preserving high contrast against pure white cards.
- **Card White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base solid and semi-translucent (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) fill for high-priority operational cards and floating metric panels.
- **Slate Text & Neutrals (`#0F172A`, `#475569`, `#94A3B8`):** Strict, legible typography hierarchies conforming to WCAG AAA contrast standards against all light backgrounds.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic family across all roles. Its geometric construction, contemporary open counters, and Indonesian origin bring both functional legibility and authentic regional cultural synergy.

- **Hero & Primary Headings:** Rendered in Extra Bold (`800`) and Bold (`700`) with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern SaaS confidence without sacrificing public sector gravitas.
- **Metric Figures:** Emphasized via bold numerical weights, paired directly above uppercase label tags for instantaneous scanning on analytics dashboards.
- **Body & Editorial:** Normal (`400`) weight configured with comfortable vertical rhythm (`lineHeight: 1.5 - 1.6`) ensures extended policy documents, curriculum guidelines, and school inspection reports remain fatigue-free.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Set in Semi-Bold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) with slight positive tracking for badges, status chips, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an adaptable **12-Column Fluid Grid** with structured viewport limits:
- **Maximum Width:** 1280px for standard portal dashboards; 1440px for expansive data analysis sheets.
- **Hero Staging:** A distinctive wide-radiused curved header section reminiscent of the reference hero card carousel, hosting floating key-metric pills and rapid-access school indicators.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Dashboard sidebars fixed at 260px.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px outer margin. Metrics and card decks compress into 2-column modules.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer margin. Interactive cards reflow into horizontal scrolling snapshots or single-column stacked rows.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth conveys institutional clarity and layered responsiveness rather than theatrical decoration:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Crisp Canvas (`#F4F8FC`) base, with structural separators using 1px borders in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 1 (Standard Surface Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0px 2px 4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04), 0px 8px 16px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass & Metrics Carousel):** Translucent card layer (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px);`), framed by a crisp border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on top and `rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06)` on bottom. Casts an atmospheric shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 132, 201, 0.10), 0px 4px 8px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Popovers):** Solid White, reinforced by a dramatic ambient shadow: `0px 24px 48px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.16)`.

## Shapes
A unified **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary reinforces an approachable, high-end educational environment:
- **Card Containers:** `rounded-2xl` (16px / `1rem`) for statistical summaries, school profile tiles, and operational feeds.
- **Hero Outer Shields:** `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px) matching the reference's soft, curved-edge framing.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, and Selectors:** `rounded-xl` (10px to 12px) for balanced tactility and touch target ergonomics.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for categorizing accreditation statuses (Akreditasi A/B/C), school levels (SD, SMP), and operational states (Aktif, Sinkron, Pending).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background Primary Cyan (`#0084C9`), text White (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, height 44px (48px for hero CTAs), `rounded-xl`, subtle cyan glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(0, 132, 201, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Glassmorphic or solid white background, text Deep Navy (`#0A2540`), 1px border `#CBD5E1`. On hover: border-color `#0084C9` with tinted background `rgba(0, 132, 201, 0.04)`.
- **Accent (Tut Wuri Action):** Radiant Accent Gold (`#FFC72C`) background with Deep Navy (`#0A2540`) text and bold weight, reserved for high-impact actions like "Verifikasi Dapodik" or "Unduh Laporan BOS".

### Metric Cards & Floating Panels
- Designed to replicate the floating arc cards from the reference image.
- Background: Translucent white with backdrop blur (`blur-md`), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, rounded-2xl padding of 20px.
- Structure: Icon badge at the top right, large bold figure (`metric-stat`), followed by descriptive label and trend chip (+5.2% vs semester lalu).

### Chips & Status Badges
- Compact `rounded-full` badges with `label-sm` typography and soft tinted backgrounds:
  - *Active / Terverifikasi:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`.
  - *Perlu Tindakan / Reviu:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#D97706`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
  - *Kecamatan / Jenjang Tag:* Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#0084C9`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 132, 201, 0.2)`.

### Form Inputs & Dropdowns
- Height 44px, `rounded-xl`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- Focus state: border `#0084C9`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 132, 201, 0.15)`, text `#0F172A`.
- Placeholder: `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18px x 18px. Active check uses Primary Cyan `#0084C9` fill with crisp white vector checkmark. Border radius 4px for checkboxes, full circle for radios.

### Educational SaaS Additions
- **Dapodik Sync Status Indicator:** A live pulsating dual-ring badge denoting synchronization health between the Konawe Utara central server and Kemendikbudristek.
- **Accreditation Pill Bar:** Visual progress track detailing verified primary institutions across the regency (SD Negeri, SD Swasta, SMP Negeri) organized by district (Asera, Lasolo, Motui, Wiwirano, dll.).
